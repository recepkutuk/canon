Basics

A panel a button opens over the page, for a short task beside it: a popover with role="dialog", named by its title (aria-labelledby) or aria-label, opened by the button's popovertarget. It opens under the button and flips to fit. Clicking outside, Escape or moving focus elsewhere closes it, and focus goes back to the button; opening it moves focus to its first field.

Anatomy

Open: an optional .canon-popover-title, then anything: a form with its actions, choices, a few words and a link.

Edit link

Choices

A few choices that apply at once, such as Swatches for a text color, need no actions.

Words

A few words and a link, for help beside a control. (A tooltip shows a short name or hint on hover; a popover is opened on purpose and can hold a link.)
