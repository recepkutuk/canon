State text

Optional words for each state, in canon-choice-state after the label: canon-choice-state-off and canon-choice-state-on, one shown at a time. The label moves above and stays the switch's name, so it never changes; the words are aria-hidden="true", because assistive tech already announces on and off. Write the label as the setting and the words as its value. Click each one to see the words change.
