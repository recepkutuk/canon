Meaning: data-intent

Use data-intent when the color means something: a status. Statuses can look different from categories (here, rounder and in capitals), so the two are never confused. High, medium and low emphasis, then with a dot.

Identity: data-seed

Use data-seed when the color only tells tags apart: categories, labels, people. Pass a stable number (an id); it always picks the same color, the same one an avatar with that seed gets. High, medium and low emphasis.
