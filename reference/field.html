Field

A form control with its label, description and messages: every box (text input, select, combobox, date, color, swatches, code, slider…) sits in a field the same way. Specification: docs/components/field.md

Basics

Inside a field, text inputs always have a placeholder attribute: empty, or with an example value.

Every control

Each box in a field: the label, its description and the message work the same way for every control. A control that is two inputs (a range) or a set of choices (swatches) names a group: the label is a <span id> and the field (or the swatches) is a role="group" labelled by it.

Required and optional

Add native required to the input and the label shows the marker. "(optional)" is your own text, inside the label so it is read with the name. Every part of a label: Label.

Label beside the box

data-label="start" on a form or any container puts every field's label beside its box, in a column a third wide (--canon-label-width), level with the box's first line. Descriptions stay under the label; messages and counters go under the box. A Fieldset's legend takes the label column, and a single checkbox or switch lines up with the boxes. With less than 32rem of room the labels go back above: drag the frame's corner to try it. On one field, data-label="start" always applies; data-label="above" inside puts a field back. Suited to forms people edit and scan (account details, settings, a record's properties); a form filled in once, top to bottom, reads faster with labels above.

On computers with a mouse, the list opens as a styled dropdown; touch devices keep the native picker. Options can hold markup (a label and a description). Character counter

An empty <p class="canon-field-counter"> after the message: canon.js shows how much of the control's maxlength is used, at the end of the message's row. With a counter, the limit doesn't stop typing or cut pasted text: people can go past it, the characters that are too many are marked in red, and the field shows its error at once, until they shorten it. (Without canon.js, maxlength stops typing as usual.) The last tenth of the limit is marked, and screen readers hear what is left after a pause in typing. Say the limit in the description, and give the field a message for the error.

Size

Expected length
