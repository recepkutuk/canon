Range Slider

A from–to range picked on one track. Specification: docs/components/range-slider.md · single thumb: Slider

Basics

Two native range inputs on one track, each with its own name. The label names the pair: the field is role="group" with aria-labelledby, and each input names its thumb. canon.js fills between the thumbs, keeps them from crossing and keeps the values in step. Each value appears over its own thumb while you hover, focus or drag that thumb. When both show (one thumb focused, the other hovered) and the thumbs are close, the two values move apart.
