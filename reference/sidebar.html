Sidebar

The column beside an app's pages: its name, its main links, and what belongs at the bottom. It collapses to a rail of icons. Specification: docs/components/sidebar.md

Basics

A .canon-sidebar: the column beside an app's pages. A .canon-sidebar-header first (a .canon-sidebar-brand: a mark and a .canon-sidebar-brand-name, as a link home; here also its toggle), one or more Nav Lists, and an optional .canon-sidebar-footer at the bottom. It scrolls on its own when its links don't fit. A link can carry a Tag after its label ("New" on Quotes), as in a Nav List; in a rail it is hidden visually and stays in the link's name. Try the toggle.

Rail

The toggle (a Button with command="--canon-toggle-sidebar", aria-controls and aria-expanded, named "Sidebar") collapses it to a rail: data-display="icon" on the sidebar. One toggle, in the same place either way: in an App Shell the bar's start; without a bar, the sidebar's header (in a rail, under the mark). Icons only, the brand's mark only; names stay for screen readers, and each link's Tooltip shows them on hover and focus (tooltips in a Nav List show only in a rail). A section's sub-pages open beside it: click Scrumboard. They close when you choose one, press Esc, or click or Tab elsewhere.
