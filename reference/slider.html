Slider

A value picked from a range. Specification: docs/components/slider.md

Basics

A Field around a native range input. canon.js fills the track up to the thumb and keeps the shown value in step; without JavaScript the slider still works, unfilled, and the value is hidden. The value appears over the thumb while you hover, focus or drag the slider (try the second one).
