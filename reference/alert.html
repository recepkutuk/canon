Alert

A message in the page that stays while it's true: about the record, the form or the setting it sits with. Specification: docs/components/alert.md

Basics

A <div class="canon-alert"> with a .canon-alert-title, a .canon-alert-description, or both. data-intent says what it means, in the same words as a Button's or a Toast's: info, success, warning, danger. The theme draws the icon, and screen readers hear the intent's word first ("Warning: This invoice is overdue"). With no intent it's a plain note.

Parts

Actions that deal with it go after the text: small high neutral Buttons, or Links. Under 20rem they go under the text. A title alone, or a description alone, is enough for one sentence. The description can be a <div> holding paragraphs or a list.

Emphasis

data-emphasis="high" fills it with the intent's color, in Button's words, for the alert that must not be missed: the page can't be used until it's dealt with. Keep it rare; one per page at most.

Dismissing

A close button (.canon-alert-close, empty and last) for an alert that can be put away without fixing anything: a tip, news. canon.js names it ("Dismiss"), fires canon-dismiss on the alert (cancelable) and hides it; focus that was in it moves on. An alert that reports a problem has none: it goes when the problem does.

Edge cases

Right to left, the icon and the intent's mark are on the right, and the intent's word is the page's language. Long words wrap; in a narrow place the actions wrap under each other.
