Lines, stripes, hover, frame

On the frame: data-lines="grid" adds lines between columns ("none" drops the ones between rows); data-striped fills every other row; data-hover highlights the row under the pointer; data-frame="none" drops the frame, so the table sits on the page. They combine.

Edge cases

Long text wraps in its cell; header cells stay on one line, and so does a cell with data-wrap="none" (the Title column here), widening the table if it must. An empty cell stays empty. Right to left, the columns read from the right and "end" is the left.
