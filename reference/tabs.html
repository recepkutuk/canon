Tabs

The parts of one page or record, one shown at a time. Specification: docs/components/tabs.md

Basics

In-page tabs: a .canon-tabs around a named strip (role="tablist") of tab buttons and their panels. Click a tab, or use the arrow keys (Home and End to the ends); the strip is one Tab stop, then the panel. The strip is a track as wide as its tabs; a raised card marks the current one and glides to the next. Not a Segmented Control: that sets a value, Tabs show a part.

Links

When each part has its own address (/invoices/2041/payments), the tabs are links: a named <nav class="canon-tabs-list">, the current one aria-current="page". No tab roles: they go somewhere. They work without JavaScript.

Narrow

Tabs that don't fit go into a More menu, from the last, one at a time; the current tab always stays. Choose one from the menu and it takes its place. Drag the frame's corner to try it.

Edge cases

Right to left, the tabs run from the right and the arrow keys follow. A tab that can't be chosen yet is aria-disabled="true": still announced, skipped by the arrows. Icons only (data-display="icon"), each keeps its text as its name.
