Split Pane

Two panes side by side, or one over the other, and a handle to share the room between them. Specification: docs/components/split-pane.md

Basics

A .canon-split-pane: a pane, a handle and a pane. The handle is a separator people can move: role="separator", tabindex="0", named, aria-controls the first pane, its size in percent (aria-valuenow) between aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax. Drag it, or focus it and use the arrows (Shift for bigger steps), Home and End for the limits, Enter to fold the first pane to its minimum and back. A double click goes back to where it started. Each pane scrolls on its own.
