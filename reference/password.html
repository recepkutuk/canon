Password

A password box with show / hide, and, for new passwords, a strength meter and the rules the password must meet. Specification: docs/components/password.md.

Most examples stand alone, named with aria-label; the live example at the end uses Fields, with labels and messages.

Box × size

Empty, hidden and shown. The eye button switches the input between type="password" and type="text" and sets aria-pressed (canon.js).

Strength

A native <meter> from 0 to 4: empty, weakest, weak, medium, strong. Your app scores the password and sets value and the word in aria-valuetext.

Strength word

Optional: the word under the bar, in the bar's color. Mark it aria-hidden="true": the meter already says it to screen readers.

Rules

Each rule is pending, met (data-state="met") or failed after a submit (data-state="error"). Screen readers hear "Met:" or "Not met:" before each one.

Live, with canon.js

Everything canon.js adds, working:

  • Rules with data-rule are checked as you type, and the form can't be submitted until they are met.
  • With data-reveal="focus", the rules open while they matter and close once they are all met.
  • With data-score="rules", the meter is scored from the rules met.
  • The sparkles button generates a strong password and fills the confirmation too.
  • Turn on Caps Lock while typing to see the warning.
  • The confirmation (data-match) turns green when it matches.
  • Problems show as each field's error message: submit, or leave a changed field that is still wrong.
