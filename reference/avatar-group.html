Spotlight

data-spotlight on the group: the avatar under the pointer comes forward (on top, a little bigger, no longer cut by the one before it) while the others fade, and its name shows in a Tooltip. Keyboards reach them too: the group is one Tab stop, and the arrow keys move between its avatars. The name comes from the avatar (its fallback's aria-label); for more, point the avatar at your own Tooltip with interestfor, as the last one does with the rest's names.
