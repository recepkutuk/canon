Spotlight

data-spotlight on an avatar: under the pointer or with focus it grows a little and shows its name in a Tooltip; it takes focus so keyboards reach it. For several avatars, put it on the Avatar Group. For more than the name, point the avatar at your own Tooltip with interestfor.
