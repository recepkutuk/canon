Card

A framed box: one thing, or one part of a page, set apart. Specification: docs/components/card.md

Basics

A .canon-card: a header (a heading as its title, a description, actions) and a body. A <section> when it is a part of the page, an <article> when it stands on its own (one of many, in a list or a grid).

With a footer

A footer under a line: actions about the whole card. data-align="end" on an item sends it and what follows to the far end, as in a Dialog. A card can be the form itself.
