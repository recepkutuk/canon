Page Header

The top of a page: what it is, what state it's in, what you can do here. Specification: docs/components/page-header.md

Basics

A <header class="canon-page-header"> at the top of a page's <main>, under the Top Bar: what stays the same on every page goes in the bar, what belongs to this page goes here. A collection's page: its name as the title, a line of facts, and its actions, the primary one (data-emphasis="high") last. At most one primary action, and only when one action is what the page is for.

A view to read

A page only for reading (a log, a report) has no primary action: its actions are all neutral, or there are none. The primary action is for when one thing is what the page is for.

Rename in place

A Button with command="--canon-edit" aimed at the title edits it where it is: Enter or clicking away keeps the change, Esc drops it. canon-edit fires on the title first (detail.value, detail.previous); cancel it to keep the old name (when saving fails, say).

Sticky

data-sticky: the header stays under the Top Bar while the page scrolls (scroll inside the frame), its back link or trail tucked away above it, so the title and the actions stay in sight. Once stuck it takes a line under it. It reaches across the page's side padding: set --canon-page-inset to it.

Edge cases

Right to left, the back link points the other way and the actions sit on the left. A page that needs no visible title (a dashboard named by its sidebar link) keeps one for screen readers, visually hidden.
