Stat

One number that matters, with what it measures and how it moved: the headline numbers of a dashboard, also called a KPI. Specification: docs/components/stat.md

Basics

A .canon-stat: a .canon-stat-label (what is measured, with its period), a .canon-stat-value (the number, formatted by your app), and optionally a .canon-stat-change and a .canon-stat-description. It is plain, with no frame of its own: the Card, the row or the page around it frames it.

The change

Your app writes the whole phrase, in the reader's language, with the direction in words: the arrow is decoration. data-direction (up, down, flat) draws the arrow; data-intent says whether the move is good (success), bad (danger), near a limit (warning) or worth noticing (info). The two are separate: an error rate going up is bad, a headcount going up is neither.

A row of stats

.canon-stats lays stats side by side, as many columns as fit (each at least 12rem), wrapping onto more rows. Name it (role="group", aria-label) when the stats share a context the page doesn't give. data-lines draws a line between the stats of a row. Here in a Card, which frames them; drag the frame's corner to see them wrap.

Sparkline

A .canon-stat-chart holds a sparkline your app draws: an inline <svg> with preserveAspectRatio="none" that strokes and fills with currentColor. Canon stretches it to the stat's width, keeps its lines their drawn width and colors it: quiet grey, or the change's color at 60%. It is aria-hidden="true" when the change already says the trend. Canon doesn't draw charts.

Loading and empty

While the value loads: aria-busy="true" on the stat (or the row) and a Skeleton block in the value. No value: an empty .canon-stat-value shows a dash, which screen readers hear as "Not set". How fresh the number is goes in the description, in your words.

Edge cases

A long label and change wrap; a value keeps its digits together and wraps only at spaces. Right to left, the stat starts on the right and the arrows mirror, as time runs the other way.
