Breadcrumb

The trail of pages above the current one, each a link back up. Specification: docs/components/breadcrumb.md

Basics

Where the page sits: a <nav class="canon-breadcrumb"> named "Breadcrumb", an <ol> of the pages above it, each a link, and the current page last, marked aria-current="page". The separators are drawn between items.

Home and collapsed pages

The first item can be an icon (house) with its name in visually hidden text. Pages in the middle can collapse into a .canon-breadcrumb-more button that opens a Menu of them, its items links (<a role="menuitem">).
