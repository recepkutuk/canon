Banner

A message about the whole app, across the top of every page while it's true. Specification: docs/components/banner.md

Basics

A <div class="canon-banner"> first in what holds the app, before the App Shell: a title, a description beside it, actions and a close button at the end. canon.js keeps it in view: it sticks to the top, and the shell's sidebar and Top Bar stick under it. Scroll the frame to try it; dismiss it and the shell moves back up.
